# CWP 1.5's residential zoning (master's pmg_residential_areas, common/production_methods/06_urban_center.txt): most of
# CWP 1.5's housing was built in its cities. CWP 1.5's urban centers turned services into housing one for one; their
# services were in surplus (around 60% below base price from 1946 to 1952), so they zoned as far as their urban form
# allowed. The current urban centers make deposits and the service sectors make services, so zoning adds housing as far
# as the urban center's amenities allow (CWP 1.5's industrial, modernist, then neo-traditional or sustainable cities),
# arcologies also needing their technology. An urban center level has 6,000 workers, CWP 1.5's 9,500, so a level houses
# two thirds of CWP 1.5's 15, 30, 45 or 60: the same housing per city worker.
